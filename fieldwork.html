Fieldwork

Expeditions to measure methane in the deep ocean

FKt241011: Canyons, Vents, and Seeps of the Chile Margin

Sailed aboard R/V Falkor(too) along the Chilean Margin from Valparaíso to Puerto Montt to Punta Arenas. Mapped seep sites from El Quisco to Mocha Island, explored multiple submarine canyons, and quantified methane fluxes across the sediment seawater interface. Read coverage of this cruise from Schmidt Ocean Institute and the cruise report for Leg 1 from NOAA.
