Teaching

Pedagogy Seminar

As an MIT Teaching Development Fellow, I designed and taught a seminar on pedagogy practices in ocean science for graduate students and postdocs at WHOI who have little access to teaching training or practical teaching opportunities. This course was so well-received that the MIT Teaching and Learning Lab asked me to generalize the seminar and package it as a “seminar in a box” to be used as a template for similar programs across departments at MIT. If you are interested in utilizing this template for a pedagogy seminar in your own department or institution, please get in contact.
